Turvajätkät

Description

Turvajätkät is a security management corporation who specialize in selling security solutions. I was commissioned to provide a simple web-site for their company using wordpress. The site includes information about the company itself, their services and contact details.


The website is built using a pre-made wordpress theme, with customizations made to fit the company's branding and requirements.


Key Features

The commissioner requested a simple and clean web-site to showcase their services and contact information. I implemented a responsive design so that the site looks good on both desktop and mobile devices, as well as contact form to allow potential clients to reach out easily.

Development Status

Currently this project is live and in use by the commissioner. I did not stay to maintain the site after delivery since the commissioner had their own internal IT team for that purpose.
